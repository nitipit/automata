Connection & privacy

Disconnected · no remote connection

Pairing, transport connection, target presence and agent admission are separate. Reconnect and Send are explicit; saved events are never replayed.

Disconnect keeps approval and drafts. Remove browser approval revokes this browser's cookie session and disconnects, but does not erase drafts or stop the agent. A Pair already submitted may finish even after closing this dialog; it cannot connect automatically.

Cookies are HttpOnly, never saved with drafts. Requester binding uses short-lived sessionStorage to survive refresh, never draft snapshots. Tabs may share cookies or clone the request binding; this is not tab isolation. Local HTTP is not encrypted or a sandbox against same-user processes or same-origin scripts.
