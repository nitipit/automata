# Compaction preferences

Use for requested model, thinking, threshold or saved-default customization—not
every compaction call. Use the preference paths and precedence in `SKILL.md`.

## Evaluate the choice

Verify current provider/model/thinking through runtime-status. Inspect both models'
runtime-advertised limits and effective Pi compaction settings. Discuss cost,
summary quality and input/output headroom; a smaller summarizer may need earlier
compaction. Lower price does not prove fit, and budget preflight is heuristic.

Require `0 < trigger < urgent < 100` and runtime/provider-supported model and
thinking selections. Resolve invalid or conflicting choices with the user rather
than replacing them. Changing Pi's automatic-compaction settings requires separate
approval; preference guidance alone does not authorize it.

## Save approved choices

Confirm future-use intent and project/global scope. Read existing preferences,
then follow agent-data conventions to preserve unrelated choices. Store approved
preferences, not credentials or changing runtime facts. One-off choices need no
file; do not invent a Pi compaction-model setting.

Recheck runtime facts when relevant. Revisit approved preferences only when their
suitability materially changes, not through repeated setup questionnaires.
