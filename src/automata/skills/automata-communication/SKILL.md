# Automata Communication

Create shared understanding, not just messages. This skill guides the interaction;
permission, assignments, reporting obligations and delivery remain with their owners.

1. **Choose the recipient and outcome.** Identify whom you represent, who needs the
   information and what the interaction should achieve. Confirm material identity
   or authority uncertainty; a reachable party is not necessarily the right recipient.
   Do not imply commitments you cannot make, guess identities or broadcast to
   compensate for uncertainty. Discussion and acknowledgement do not grant permission.

2. **Choose a suitable channel.** Use an authorized path suited to privacy, audience,
   availability, urgency and reply needs. Respect reporting and isolation boundaries;
   if no suitable path exists, explain the gap and agree on an alternative. Saved
   addresses or working connections do not prove identity or permission to disclose.
   Human-readable or structured formats can suit either humans or agents. For
   terminal delivery, verify the exact endpoint and receiving input contract, and
   keep one writer through submission. Never overwrite human drafts, send messages
   into a shell/editor, or dismiss prompts to make delivery work. Paste alone is
   not submission; inspect partial delivery before retrying.

3. **Provide necessary context.** State the purpose and include only what the receiver
   needs. Do not assume shared history, file access or terminology; provide usable
   references or essential facts inline. Separate facts, assumptions and unknowns.
   Minimize private data. Explain meaningful changes and their impact without repeating
   unchanged status or turning every update into a request. When a response matters,
   make the required response, recipient, return path and timing clear.

4. **Check the outcome.** Distinguish sending, delivery, acknowledgement, understanding
   and completed action; claim only what evidence supports. Avoid acknowledgement
   loops. Leave receipts, delivery failures and retries to the transport owner.
   Retain only accepted decisions or needed evidence through existing state owners.

5. **Repair misunderstandings.** Identify the specific context or assumption mismatch
   without blame. State the correction and its consequence; confirm only when remaining
   uncertainty affects the next action. Check context and channel constraints rather
   than repeating the message more forcefully. Aim for understanding, not manipulation.
